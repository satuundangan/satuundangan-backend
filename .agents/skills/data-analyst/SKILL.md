# Lead Data & Growth Analyst Specialist (SatuUndangan)

This skill equips the agent with domain expertise in business metrics, conversion funnels, and data analytics for SatuUndangan.

---

## 🎯 Core Focus Areas

### 1. Funnel & Conversion Tracking
- **Funnel Stages**:
  $$\text{Visitor} \longrightarrow \text{Studio Editor} \longrightarrow \text{Preview} \longrightarrow \text{Checkout} \longrightarrow \text{Paid Order (Settlement)}$$
- **Drop-off Identification**: Pinpoint high-friction steps (e.g. form fatigue, photo upload errors, payment hesitation).
- **Payment Success Rate**: Monitor Midtrans settlement rate vs. expired/cancelled transactions.

### 2. Pricing & Tier Analysis
- **Pricing Strategy (2026)**:
  - **Basic**: Rp 49.000 (Entry level, high volume, no watermark).
  - **Premium**: Rp 79.000 (Best Seller target, 20 photos, custom subdomain).
  - **Eksklusif**: Rp 99.000 (Maximum tier, 35 photos, priority support).
- **Metric Targets**: Average Order Value (AOV), Revenue per Visitor (RPV), Promo code usage and elasticity.

### 3. Template Performance Ranking
- Track which template slugs drive the most creations and checkout conversions.
- Flag low-performing templates for redesign or repositioning.

### 4. Viral Loops & Organic Growth
- Measure the **Viral Coefficient ($K$-factor)** generated by the footer badge (*"Dibuat dengan SatuUndangan"*):
  $$K = i \times c$$
  *(where $i$ is invitations viewed per guest, and $c$ is conversion to new invitation creation).*
- RSVP engagement: Confirmations, virtual envelope (digital gift) transfer frequency, and guest wishes sentiment.
